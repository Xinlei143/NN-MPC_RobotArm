# Frozen FullVirtual Rerun

Twenty FullVirtual cases were rerun from a detached, clean frozen worktree.
Their case-level TCP RMSE exactly matched the historical compatible cases
(mean 28.3719 mm; maximum absolute case difference 0). The refreshed matched
ablation confirms future-alignment benefit (+2.820 mm) and the necessity of
reanchoring (+654.596 mm), while the no-feedback interval crosses zero.
